# Technology wordmarks

The landing page's **Powered by** marquee identifies technologies used by Figart.
It does not represent customers, sponsors, or endorsements.

The SVG wordmarks in `public/brands/` are original artwork sourced from the
[SVGL collection](https://github.com/pheralb/svgl/tree/main/static/library):

The collection's MIT notice is preserved in [svgl-LICENSE.txt](svgl-LICENSE.txt).
